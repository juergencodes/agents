# Frame Requirements

Use this skill when requirements need to be established or clarified, especially when they are not yet sufficiently understood, the elicitation is itself difficult, or a requirements artifact will be valuable for later design, implementation, review, or handoff.

Requirements may be developed from an existing problem frame, but a full problem-framing step is not mandatory if the problem is already clear enough for the agreed purpose.

## Approach

Read [guides/requirements-engineering.md](guides/requirements-engineering.md) for the detailed approach. Consult [examples/requirements.md](examples/requirements.md) when a concrete example would help.

Make requirements as complete as needed for the agreed scope and purpose; do not claim absolute completeness. Distinguish confirmed requirements from assumptions and open questions. Assign stable, unique IDs to requirements and preserve useful references to problem-frame IDs.

## Revisit requirements

Requirements are not necessarily final before solution work begins. If solution analysis, implementation, or testing reveals a material gap, conflict, or invalid assumption, explain the signal and likely impact, then propose revisiting the affected requirements and, when needed, the related problem frame. Do not silently proceed on a known unreliable basis.
