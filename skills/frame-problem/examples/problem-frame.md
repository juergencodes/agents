# Example: Problem Frame

## Purpose

Understand why volunteer coordinators struggle to organize recurring events. This frame is intended to guide discovery; it does not select or prescribe a product solution.

## Context

Small volunteer organizations currently coordinate events using a mix of spreadsheets and group chats.

## Problem aspects

- **P-1 — Coordination information is distributed:** Event details are kept in multiple places. It is not yet known how often this causes errors.
- **P-2 — Participation is difficult to predict:** Coordinators report uncertainty about attendance. The scale and causes have not been independently verified.

## Affected parties

- **A-1 — Volunteer coordinators:** Responsible for organizing events and communicating changes.
- **A-2 — Volunteers:** Need timely, clear information about event plans.

## Constraints and assumptions

- **K-1 — Limited administrative time:** Assumption based on the organization size; needs confirmation.

## Open questions

- **Q-1:** Which coordination failures occur most often, and what evidence is available?
- **Q-2:** Do volunteers and coordinators describe the main difficulty in the same way?

The IDs let later research or requirements refer to specific aspects without treating uncertain claims as established facts.
