# Example: Independent Assessment

Main task: choose a direction for a product concept. The main conversation has developed a preferred option, but an independent critique may reveal overlooked assumptions.

The assistant proposes a side investigation and provides a prompt such as:

```text
Assess whether the following product concept addresses a real need for its stated audience. Identify the strongest supporting considerations, the most important assumptions or risks, and what evidence would change your assessment.

Concept: A lightweight planning tool for small volunteer organizations.
Audience: Volunteer coordinators who currently organize recurring events with spreadsheets and group chats.
Constraint: The initial product must be feasible for a small team to build and maintain.

Please assess this independently. Do not assume the concept should succeed. A useful outcome may be a qualified assessment or a conclusion that the available information is insufficient; do not invent evidence. Keep the response concise and separate observations from assumptions.
```

The user can start a separate chat with the prompt and choose which findings, if any, to bring back. A useful result might identify a key assumption to validate; a conclusion that the concept cannot be assessed from the supplied information is also valid.
